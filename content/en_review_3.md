- tm-youil: hook "…share Europe's roots" → "Korean and Japanese weekdays match Europe's". No day name shares a word root with a European one; what matches is the scheme (sun, moon, five planets, same order), which is all the reason line supports. KO hook "같은 뿌리" overstates in the same way and should be reworded (e.g. "같은 짝"/"같은 방식").
- ct-bistro: hook "The ‘bistro means quick’ story is on shaky ground" → "Bistro is Russian for ‘quick’? That story's shaky". The old cloze blanked the whole phrase, leaving "The ‘___’ story is on shaky ground" with no topic word. Now the blank is the recallable word "quick" and the verdict stays visible. Hedge unchanged (근거가 약하다 = shaky).
- ct-yeonpil: hook "The pencil (鉛筆) is named for lead…" → "鉛筆 (pencil) is named for lead but has none". The old wording read as if English "pencil" is named for lead, which contradicts ct-pencil (pencil < penicillus).
- tm-lent: hook "In Old English, spring was ‘Lent’" → "English once called spring ‘Lent’". The Old English form was lencten (the strip shows it), not Lent. KO "옛 영어" is looser and fine.
- tm-mardi: reason "His day became Tuesday (mardi, martes)" → "His day is Tuesday (mardi, martes, 火曜日)". This restores the KO link (화요일 = Mars/화성's day) that explains the 火曜日 strip item. "became" → "is" to match KO.
- ct-pen: reason "That's why Spanish pluma…" → "For the same reason, Spanish pluma…". Pluma/Feder mean pen because of quills, not because of Latin penna. The old "That's why" could be read the wrong way.
- fd-shirt: reason "It split…" → "The word split…", and "as garments cut short" → "a garment cut short". Clearer subject and smoother phrasing; hedge ("One theory") unchanged.
